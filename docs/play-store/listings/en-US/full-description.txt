Qetoret is a Christian prayer companion for bringing what is on your heart before God, returning to it with intention and remembering answers over time.

BEGIN WITH A PRAYER
Start a quiet prayer moment without creating an account. When you choose to save your prayer, sign in to keep it in your personal journal.

KEEP A PRAYER JOURNAL
Write requests for yourself or others, add updates and prayer notes, and record answered prayers and testimonies. Organize what you carry in prayer through circles such as your household, church and the nations.

BUILD A RHYTHM
Find the prayers due today, set a recurring rhythm and plan follow-ups. Use the calendar to see what is coming and enable optional reminders.

GROW THROUGH SCRIPTURE
Explore guided prayer plans, Bible passages and readings about prayer and the Christian life. Walk through a plan at your own pace and keep personal reflections along the way.

PRAY TOGETHER
Create or join prayer groups, share selected requests with their members, carry one another's prayers and encourage each other with updates. Choose what to share and with whom.

CAPTURE MORE THAN WORDS
Add photos, voice recordings or video to supported updates and testimonies. Record a note during a prayer session and return to it later.

PRIVACY AND RECOVERY
Private journal content is encrypted in your browser before upload. Shared group content is encrypted for its members, who can read and keep what you share. Set up optional vault recovery before changing devices or browsers so you can unlock your encrypted journal there.

You can start as a guest. An account is needed for syncing and community features. Some journal actions work offline and sync when a connection returns; sign-in, media uploads and other online features need internet access.

Qetoret is free to use and available in 16 interface languages, including English and French.
