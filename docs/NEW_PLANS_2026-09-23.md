## How to review

1. Open any build with `?planPreview=1` (device-local review mode; `?planPreview=0`
   switches it off). Drafts appear on the Plans page with a "review pending"
   badge and can be started and read day by day.
2. Read the plan's research note, `docs/plans/<id>.md`: structure, passages
   considered and rejected, disputed readings, safety days, and what the
   theology and safety reviewers must check.
3. To publish a plan, a named human replaces `pendingPlanReview('<id>')` with a
   dated record in a new file under `src/content/reviews/` (model:
   `paulWisdom20260908.js`): `status: 'approved'`, theology, safety and all 16
   locale sign-offs, each with reviewer name and ISO date. **Only a human may
   write that record.** Earlier approvals do not cover these plans.

## Owner decisions these drafts follow

- The "Growing in Christ" plans read the existing `christian-living` resource
  domain (no separate `formation` domain); a new plan category `formation`
  ("Growing in Christ") groups them.
- Man/Woman of God keep to what Christians share; where a text raises
  headship, submission or church office (Ephesians 5, 1 Peter 3, Romans 16,
  Deborah, Acts 2), the plan names the complementarian and egalitarian readings
  fairly and does not decide between them.
- Holy Spirit: a Pentecostal voice (being filled with the Spirit is real and
  may be sought); Spirit baptism after conversion and tongues are presented as
  the Pentecostal understanding, with other readings named; no "initial
  evidence" claim; never "no tongues = no Spirit".
- Already-approved books on the `christian-living` shelf that sit uneasily with
  a plan's guardrails are shown and flagged below, not hidden.

## Architecture (shared, all additive)

- Plan category `formation`; key `planCategoryFormation` in 16 locales.
- Resource domains `intercession` (praying for a loved one who has wandered),
  `mission` (praying for and witnessing to people who don't yet believe) and
  `care` (church hurt, spiritual abuse, safeguarding — kept apart from the
  deliverance shelf). `intercession` and `mission` are separate because the two

  spiritual-gifts, spirit-baptism, prophecy*, psalms, lament, lords-prayer,
  justice, sabbath, church-hurt*, spiritual-abuse* (* sensitive).

  (`src/content/dailyVerses.js`): Judges, Ruth, 2 Kings (ru "4 Царств"), Ezra,

  `src/content/plans/testing/newPlanContract.js`; each plan's test adds its own

**Theology calls**
- work21 leaves out 2 Thessalonians 3:10 on purpose (easily heard as shaming
  unemployed or disabled readers).
- womanOfGod21 day 15 does not name Junia (Romans 16:7); no day on Hannah
  (infertility deliberately kept out).
- kingdomCome14: the doxology is not used; does day 1 ("Our Father") need a
  safety note for readers with painful fathers?
- holySpirit21 day 18 wording on Spirit baptism; identity21 day 5 (Romans 7).
- churchHurt21 day 14 reads Matthew 18:15-17 as a process between equals, not
  for abuse; Psalm 105:15 ("touch not my anointed") is not addressed.
- psalms42: Psalm 146 (day 42, with 145 related); should Psalm 110 join week 5?

**Safety and wording**
- "Must be reported" and "safeguarding lead" are UK-flavoured; legal duties and
  helplines differ by country (helplines are named generically everywhere).
- unborn21: days 16, 20 and 21 deliberately show no parenting books; there is no
  shortcut to the last day after a loss; the completion card itself is neutral
  ("21 days complete").
- `Jude 1:20-21`, `Jude 1:24-25` and `3 John 1:9-11` are the first references to
  single-chapter books — check them in the verse reader.

**Native review**: every non-EN/FR day title, plan title and movement name
(e.g. prodigal30 avoids the gendered "prodigal son" in several languages;
"pilgrimage" in Arabic and Swahili for psalms42).
