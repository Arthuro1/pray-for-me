## 6. Gaps

- **Amharic**: nothing verified. GotQuestions Amharic has no miscarriage or pregnancy page (sitemap checked, 283 URLs). The EOTC (Mahibere Kidusan) results were about marital ethics, not pregnancy.
- **Non-loss pregnancy prayer outside en/fr/de/es/pt**: ru, ja, ko, ar, fa, hi, id, sw and tl readers get only loss material (plus the zh postpartum and father pieces). Korean 태교 prayer books appeared only on retailer pages; the Indonesian results were lifestyle sites.
- **Postpartum mental health**: only en, pt and zh (Butler) are verified; nothing in es, fr, de or the Global South languages.
- **Birth day (19) and entrusting (21)**: no candidate is specifically about labour or dedicating the child. The §5 leads (TGC "10 Convictions", Crossway, the Ligonier Hannah devotional) are the next place to look.
- **Topic model**: there is no `stillbirth` or `postpartum` topic. Stillbirth resources use `miscarriage` (the plan's loss tag), and postpartum resources use `mental-health`.
- **Eternal-state claims**: several loss resources assert a view on the child's eternal state (Hutto, McClure, GotQuestions, TopChrétien). The plan deliberately avoids this question, so the curator should set a policy.
