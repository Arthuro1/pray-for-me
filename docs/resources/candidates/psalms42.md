  fa, hi, id, sw, tl and am the shelf has only BibleProject (and ru/zh/ja/ko/
  fa/hi/id/sw/tl/am link to language hub pages, not dedicated video pages).
  There was no native search for ja, ar, fa, hi, id, sw, tl or am, and Russian
  gave only the unhosted PDF above. African, Asian and Middle-Eastern Psalms
  material (e.g. the Africa Bible Commentary, Langham, national Bible
  societies) is still to be searched.
- **Pentecostal/charismatic voices on the Psalms**: none found or searched. The
  shelf is evangelical/Reformed/Anglican, plus Lutheran (Bonhoeffer) and
  mainline (Brueggemann).
- **Week 4 (repentance: Pss 6, 25, 32, 38, 51, 103, 130)**: only the
  BibleProject "Confession" talk and the general books. There is no dedicated
  penitential-psalms resource (a Psalm 51 piece such as Evangelium21's "Beten
  mit Psalm 51" was seen in search results but not opened).
- **Week 6 (thanksgiving, pilgrimage, Songs of Ascents)**: no dedicated
  resource. Eugene Peterson's *A Long Obedience in the Same Direction* (Pss
  120–134) is an obvious lead that was not checked.
- **Topic note**: no `imprecation` topic exists. Imprecatory pieces use
  `justice` (+ `forgiveness`/`lament`), which matches days 19 and 34.
