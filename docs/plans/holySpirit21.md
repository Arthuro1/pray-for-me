# holySpirit21 — Intimacy with the Holy Spirit — Walking in His Presence

**Status:** Drafted with AI assistance on 2026-09-23. Not reviewed by a human. EN and FR authored; the other 14 languages show AI-drafted day titles and fall back to English prose.

Files: `src/content/plans/intimacyWithTheSpirit.js` (meta), `intimacyWithTheSpiritDays.js` (21 days), `intimacyWithTheSpirit.test.js` (contract + guardrails). UI keys (`planHolySpirit*`, 16 languages) are in the scratch `i18n/holySpirit21.json` for the integrator.

## 1. Purpose and audience

A 21-day prayer plan for Pentecostal and charismatic Christians that broader evangelicals can also use. It moves from doctrine to dependence: the Spirit as God and as a Person, walking and listening, testing impressions, praying with His help, conviction without condemnation, fruit and gifts, power for witness, the church, spiritual dryness and daily fellowship. It never treats the Spirit as a force, energy, feeling or technique, and it promises no experience, gift or outcome. About 10–15 minutes a day.

## 3. Passages considered and rejected

- **Joel 2:28-32** — central to Pentecost, but English numbering (2:28) differs from Hebrew/Luther/some others (3:1), so the localized verse pipeline could show the wrong verses in de and elsewhere. Used instead through Peter's quotation, `Acts 2:14-21` (day 19), and named in prose only.
- **Ezekiel 37 (dry bones)** — tempting for the dryness day, but its subject is the national restoration of Israel; using it for personal devotional dryness would allegorize it. Psalm 63 (David in a dry land) is used instead.
- **Psalm 51:10-12** — considered for conviction (day 14). Rejected there because v11 (David asking God not to take His Spirit) can frighten a sensitive conscience without an explanation of its old-covenant setting (cf. Saul, 1 Samuel 16:14) against the Spirit's sealing in Christ (Ephesians 1:13-14). Reviewer may want to reintroduce it with that note.
- **1 Samuel 16** (anointing of David) — rejected as a primary text: the Spirit rushing on a king is a unique office-anointing and is easily turned into "impartation" teaching.
- **Acts 19:1-7** — cited in prose on day 18 rather than attached, because the Ephesian "disciples" are disputed (disciples of John, not yet Christian believers, in many readings) and it should not become a formula.
- **Isaiah 11:1-3, 44:3** — relevant (Spirit on the Messiah; Spirit poured on offspring) but crowded out by the three-related limit.
- **1 Corinthians 2:9** — used, but explicitly read with v10: the verse is about what God *has revealed* by the Spirit, not a description of heaven.
- **Acts 8:26-40** — used as narrative only; the reflection says Luke tells history, not a template for how every leading comes.

## 4. Interpretive issues and how the text handles them

- **Spirit baptism / subsequence (owner decision followed).** Day 18 presents the Pentecostal understanding in its own words ("Pentecostal Christians understand this baptism in the Spirit as an empowering experience that can follow conversion, often with speaking in tongues, as in Acts 2, 10 and 19") and names the other reading fairly ("every believer is baptised in the Spirit at conversion and then filled again and again, as in Acts 4:31"). Common ground: Luke 11:13 and power for witness, not status. A prompt invites the reader to ask the Father to fill them, trusting Him with how He answers. Ephesians 5:18 (continuous filling) appears on day 21 and in the biblical note.
- **Tongues.** Day 12 affirms tongues as a treasured prayer gift (1 Corinthians 14:14-18) and also states the plain sense of 1 Corinthians 12:30 (not all speak in tongues). Many Pentecostals read 12:30 as referring to the *public ministry* gift, distinct from a private prayer language available to all; the text does not adjudicate this. "Initial evidence" (classical Pentecostal / Assemblies of God doctrine) is deliberately **not** used anywhere in the prose, per the owner decision; a test forbids it. Days 5 and 12 state plainly that everyone who belongs to Christ has the Spirit (Romans 8:9; 1 Corinthians 12:13).
- **Romans 8:26 "groanings".** Gordon Fee argues it likely includes praying in tongues; many other commentators (e.g. Douglas Moo) read it as the Spirit's own wordless intercession. Day 11 stays with "longings deeper than words" and does not decide.
- **Praying to the Spirit.** Day 2 invites the reader to speak to the Spirit directly. Historic orthodoxy (Nicene: worshipped and glorified with the Father and Son) supports it; some evangelicals prefer the pattern "to the Father, through the Son, in the Spirit". Reviewer may soften.
- **2 Corinthians 3:13 (fading glory).** Translations differ on whether Moses hid the *fading* of glory or its *end/goal*; "a fading glory" follows the majority of modern versions.
- **Prophecy today.** Written from a continuationist stance (the owner's), with testing built in (1 Thessalonians 5:19-22; 1 Corinthians 14:29). Cessationist readers will disagree; the plan does not argue the point.

## 5. Safety concerns and handling

Safety notes on days **8, 9, 12, 13, 16, 20**:
- **8 (listening):** not every thought, dream, feeling, coincidence or inner voice is from God; nothing urging harm, stopping treatment or isolation bears Jesus' character; frightening or commanding voices → doctor as well as pastor; seeking help is not a lack of faith. The day's practice says to write impressions down and *not act yet*.
- **9 (testing):** "God told me" is never leverage; **"God told me you must marry me"** named explicitly as inconsistent with the Spirit of Jesus, alongside money, unquestioning obedience, secrecy and unwanted touch; "you are free to say no"; Praystead never gives personal prophecy; caution on words predicting marriage, pregnancy, illness, death, money, politics; pastor / trusted believer / emergency services. A selfPrompt asks the reader whether they have used "I feel led" to win an argument.
- **12 (tongues):** not a measure of worth, faith or the Spirit's presence; no pressure, shame or coaching to copy sounds; no technique produces gifts.
- **13 (dryness):** dryness is common and not failure; persistent low mood, sleep/appetite change or self-harm thoughts → doctor or counsellor; emergency services if in danger. Not diagnostic.
- **16 (gifts/prophecy):** gifts never override Scripture, consent or care; never stop treatment because of a prophetic word; caution with paid words, certainty about life events, or leaders who refuse to be weighed.
- **20 (quenching):** the warning is never a reason to silence questions; disagreeing with a leader is not resisting the Spirit; the Spirit's name used to extract money, secrecy, isolation or keep someone unsafe → another trusted leader or counsellor; emergency services.

Spiritual warfare is not a theme of this plan (no day needed a warfare note).
