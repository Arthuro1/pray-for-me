## Purpose
Biblical manhood as a life submitted to God and increasingly conformed to Christ.
NOT primarily a marriage-role plan. Deeply useful to single men, married men,
fathers, men without children, young adults, older men, leaders and men with no
formal leadership role. It must be a genuinely distinct curriculum from the
womanOfGod21 plan (another agent writes that one) — male biblical examples,
pressures, temptations and questions — not the same plan with pronouns swapped.

## Shared foundation (days 1–5 especially)
created in God's image; sinners needing grace; united with Christ; indwelt by the
Spirit; called to holiness, wisdom, courage, service; gifted; members of Christ's body.

## Themes
image of God; sonship; identity in Christ; integrity; courage; humility; emotional
honesty (Jesus wept; David's psalms); repentance; self-control; sexuality;
treatment of women; brotherhood; friendship (David and Jonathan); work;
responsibility; servant leadership; protecting without controlling; sacrificial
love; spiritual fatherhood (Paul and Timothy); suffering; perseverance; service in
the church; finishing faithfully.

## Passages
Genesis 1–2 (1:26-27, 2:15), Psalm 1, Micah 6:8, Proverbs, Mark 10:42-45, John 13,
John 11:35, 1 Corinthians 16:13-14 (courage AND love), Ephesians 5 in context
(5:21 ff — see owner decision), Philippians 2, 1 Thessalonians 2:7-12 (Paul like a
mother and a father), 1 Thessalonians 4:1-8, 1 Timothy 3 and 6 (6:11-12), Titus
2:6-8, 2 Timothy (1:5-7, 2:1-2, 22, 4:7), 1 Peter 5, 1 Samuel 18–20 (Jonathan),
2 Samuel 11–12 (David's fall — power and repentance), Joseph (Genesis 39),
Nehemiah, Daniel, Joshua 1:6-9, Job 31:1.

## Owner decision on disputed roles (follow exactly)
Shared ground only. Where a text (Ephesians 5, 1 Peter 3, 1 Timothy 2–3) raises
headship/church-office questions, name fairly that Christians read it differently
(complementarian and egalitarian readings) and do not adjudicate; keep to what
they share — Christlike love, humility, service, faithfulness, repentance,
responsibility. Flag any such day in the research note for theological review.
Tag only that day's resources with `marriage-roles` if you tag it at all.

## Avoid stereotypes (write tests)
"real men never cry"; "men must dominate"; emotional suppression; wealth as proof
of masculinity; marriage as necessary for full manhood; fatherhood as necessary for
full manhood; violence as strength; women as temptresses/objects.

## Safety
safetyNote on sexuality/pornography day (help is available: accountability,
pastor, counsellor; never shame), and on a day about anger/power (if you have
harmed someone or fear you might, seek help now; if someone is in danger, call
emergency services). Treatment of women: respect, consent, never control.
