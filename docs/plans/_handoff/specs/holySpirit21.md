## Purpose
Biblical, Christ-centred fellowship with the Holy Spirit — beyond doctrine to
dependence: indwelling presence, walking by the Spirit, praying with His help,
conviction without condemnation, holiness, attentiveness to His leading, testing
impressions, fruit, gifts, worship in Spirit and truth, power for witness,
community, perseverance through spiritual dryness, daily dependence.
The Spirit is a divine PERSON — never an impersonal force, energy, feeling or technique.

## Owner decision on doctrine (follow exactly)
Pentecostal voice: being filled with the Spirit is a real experience believers may
seek (Luke 11:13, Acts 2, 4:31, Ephesians 5:18). Present "baptism in the Spirit
as distinct from conversion" and tongues as the Pentecostal understanding, and
name fairly that other Christians read Acts differently (Spirit baptism at
conversion; filling repeated; tongues not the evidence for all). Do NOT claim
tongues are the initial evidence as Praystead's position. NEVER say or imply the
Spirit is absent from a Christian who has not spoken in tongues (Romans 8:9,
1 Corinthians 12:13, 12:30).

## Guardrails (strict — write tests for them)
- Never encourage assuming every thought, dream, emotion, coincidence, mental
  image, inner voice or bodily sensation is from the Spirit. Impressions are
  tested by Scripture, the character of Jesus, wisdom, Christian community, mature
  counsel, fruit, circumstances where appropriate, humility about uncertainty.
- Never write "God told you…". Prefer "You may sense…", "Consider prayerfully…",
  "Test this carefully…".
- The Spirit is never invoked to justify manipulation, coercion, controlling
  others, violating consent, financial exploitation, abandoning medical care,
  refusing mental-health care, illegal conduct, abuse, dangerous behaviour,
  contradicting Scripture. "God told me you must marry me" must be clearly
  inconsistent with the plan's guidance (say so explicitly on day 9 or its safety
  note). "God told me" is never leverage over another person.
- Experiences: affirm deep experiences of God's presence; do NOT make emotional
  intensity the standard of intimacy. Include at least one reflection on spiritual
  dryness (core principle: God's presence is not measured solely by our awareness
  of it) — e.g. day 13 or 20 or 21.
- Tongues (if discussed, day 12/16): Acts 2, 10, 19, 1 Corinthians 12–14. No
  shaming, no teaching imitation, no emotional manipulation, no promise that a
  technique produces a gift.
- Prophecy (day 9/16): Romans 12, 1 Corinthians 12–14, 1 Thessalonians 5:19-22;
  require discernment and testing; never generate personalized prophecy; no
  predictions about marriage, pregnancy, illness, death, finances, politics,
  future events.
- safetyNote on days about hearing God, impressions, prophecy, spiritual warfare
  or potentially dangerous guidance claims (at least days 8, 9, 12 or 16, 20).
