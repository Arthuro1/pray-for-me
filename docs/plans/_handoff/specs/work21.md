# work21 — Work, Calling & Faithfulness

## Purpose
Bring work, vocation, study, unemployment, household work, leadership, ambition,
provision, decisions and rest under Christ. Do NOT reduce calling to discovering
one secret career God has hidden. Useful for employees, employers, students,
unemployed readers, retirees, stay-at-home parents, caregivers, tradespeople,
professionals, ministry workers — every day's prompts must work for all of them
(use "your work — paid or unpaid", "your studies", etc.).

## Themes
creation and work; work after the Fall; serving Christ; diligence; excellence
without perfectionism; integrity; money and provision; ambition; comparison;
unemployment; difficult coworkers; difficult authority; leading others; hidden
work; gifts; decisions; failure; rest; Sabbath principles; generosity; witness;
surrendering career.

## Passages

Ecclesiastes (2:18-26, 3:9-13, 5:18-20), Matthew 6 (6:19-34), Matthew 20:1-16,
Matthew 25:14-30, Luke 3:10-14, Colossians 3:22-24 (see note), Ephesians 4:28,
1 Thessalonians 4:9-12, 2 Thessalonians 3:6-13, James 4:13-17, James 5:1-6
(unjust employers), 1 Timothy 6:6-10, 17-19, Exodus 20:8-11, Deuteronomy 5:12-15,
Mark 2:27, Psalm 127:1-2, Daniel 6 (integrity at work), Nehemiah 2, Acts 18:1-4
(Paul the tentmaker), Exodus 31:1-6 (Bezalel — gifted craft), 1 Corinthians 7:17-24.
Be careful with passages historically used to justify exploitation (Colossians
3:22 / Ephesians 6:5 were addressed to enslaved people in a Roman household
system; they do not endorse slavery or unjust labour; pair with Ephesians 6:9,
Colossians 4:1, James 5:4; say so on that day).

## Guardrails
- No prosperity theology: diligence and faithfulness don't guarantee wealth or
  success; poverty/unemployment isn't a sign of God's disfavour.
- No "hustle" idolatry; no guilt for rest; no shame for unemployment, illness or
  disability limiting work.
- Unemployment day: practical + pastoral; safetyNote may point to help with debt,
  despair (counsel, pastor, crisis line) briefly.
- Difficult authority/workplace day: safetyNote — harassment, abuse, unsafe or
  illegal practices at work should be reported (HR, union, authorities); submission
  to authority never requires sin or enduring abuse.
- Ministry workers: ministry is not holier than other work.
