## Purpose
Sustained intercession for someone who once identified with Christian faith or
community and has wandered away (a child, spouse, sibling, parent, friend).
Different from praying for someone who has never believed (a separate plan
exists — unbelievers30). Do not treat the person as an object to control. Do not
promise they will return during (or after) the plan.

## Themes
God's compassion; repentance; remembering truth; freedom from deception; healing
from wounds (including wounds caused by Christians or churches — some leave
because they were hurt); consequences and mercy; patient love; avoiding
manipulation; knowing when to speak; knowing when to remain quiet; forgiveness;
family pain; healthy boundaries; trusting God's timing; surrender.

## Passages
Study Luke 15 carefully in context (three parables to Pharisees; the father
watches but does not chase into the far country; the elder brother is also lost —
the intercessor's heart). Also Hosea 14, Jeremiah 3 (3:12-14, 22), James 5:19-20,
2 Timothy 2:24-26 (gentleness, God grants repentance), Psalm 51, Romans 2:4,
1 Corinthians 13, Galatians 6:1-2, 1 Peter 5:7, Luke 22:31-32 (Peter), John 21:15-19,
Psalm 34:18, Isaiah 55:6-11, 2 Peter 3:9, Ezekiel 34:11-16, Matthew 18:12-14.

## Guardrails
- Never claim knowledge of why a particular person left the faith; name several
  possible reasons (doubt, hurt, sin, deconstruction, church failure, trauma,
  mental illness, intellectual questions) without assigning any.
- Never reduce every departure to rebellion.
- No manipulation: no guilt-trips, pressure, ultimatums, "tough love" as leverage,
  covert evangelism tactics, deceiving the person.
- Boundaries: love can say no; if the person is violent, exploitative or addicted,
  protecting yourself and others is not unloving (safetyNote on that day; name
  help: pastor, counsellor, addiction/family support, police if in danger).
- Grief: the plan honours family pain and allows lament.
- Every day: `selfPrompt` mirror (the intercessor's own heart).
- Completion: no promise of return; continue praying with hope and surrender.
