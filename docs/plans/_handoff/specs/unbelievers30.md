## Purpose
Sustained biblical intercession for people who do not yet know Christ, while
shaping the believer into a faithful, loving, courageous witness. No manipulation,
no pressure tactics, no treating people as projects. Friendship is not a tactic.
The person keeps their dignity and freedom.

## Themes
God's love; Christ's saving work; repentance; spiritual understanding; hearing the
gospel; Scripture; credible Christian witness; courage; compassion; opportunities
for conversation; listening; answering questions with gentleness; local churches;
missionaries; unreached peoples; persecuted Christians; people of other religious
backgrounds (respectfully — no caricatures of other faiths); people harmed by
Christians; patience; surrender.

## Passages
Luke 15, Luke 19:10, John 3 (3:16-17), John 6 (6:44 — handle with care),
Romans 1 (1:16), Romans 10 (10:1, 13-15), 1 Corinthians 2, 2 Corinthians 4:1-6,
Ephesians 2, Colossians 4:2-6, 1 Timothy 2:1-6, 2 Peter 3:9, 1 Peter 3:15-16,
Acts 16:14 (Lydia — the Lord opened her heart), Acts 17:22-31, Matthew 9:36-38,
Romans 9:1-3 (Paul's anguish), John 4, John 17:20-23, Acts 26:18.
Handle election/predestination passages carefully: hold God's sovereignty in
salvation AND human responsibility to call and respond; do not turn the plan into a
denominational argument (note the tension in the research note).

## Guardrails
- Guard against guaranteed-conversion language ("they will be saved", "claim
  their salvation", "God will save them because you prayed").
- No spiritual-warfare formulas binding someone's mind; you may pray Acts 26:18 /
  2 Corinthians 4:4 themes soberly.
- Every day: `selfPrompt` mirror (the one praying: courage, love, integrity,
  listening, repentance for harm Christians have done).
- Persecuted-church day and other-faiths day: pray with respect; no us/them hostility.
- If the person is a family member who rejects the faith, respect boundaries.
