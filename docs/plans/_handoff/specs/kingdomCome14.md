## Purpose
Pray according to the pattern Jesus gave, especially "Your kingdom come, your will
be done, on earth as it is in heaven". Move prayer from centring on personal wants
toward God's name, kingdom, will, daily dependence, forgiveness, reconciliation,
holiness, deliverance from temptation and evil, mission, justice, mercy, Christ's
return. Praying for the kingdom = (1) asking God to act AND (2) submitting our own
lives to His reign.

## Key theological notes
- Our Father: relational and communal ("our"). Earthly fatherhood can make this
  hard; do NOT tell wounded readers to project an earthly father's character onto
  God — God is the Father Jesus reveals (Luke 15, Matthew 7:9-11).
- Kingdom = God's reign revealed in and through Jesus; explain "already and not
  yet" carefully. NEVER equate it with a party, nation, government, denomination,
  social movement, material prosperity or cultural domination.
- Your will: Gethsemane — surrender without emotional denial (Jesus was
  "overwhelmed with sorrow").
- Daily bread: dependence, generosity, the plural "us".
- Forgiveness: does not necessarily mean immediate trust, reconciliation or
  unsafe access (safetyNote on day 9).
- Temptation and evil: sober, biblical spiritual-warfare framing; do not diagnose
  unseen causes; mention that "evil"/"the evil one" is a translation question.
- Doxology ("for yours is the kingdom…") is absent from the earliest manuscripts —
  mention gently if you use it, don't build a day on it.
- Come, Lord Jesus: Christ's return, resurrection, final justice, renewal of
  creation, end of death, God dwelling with His people. Not date-setting.
