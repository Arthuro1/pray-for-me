## Purpose
Foundational discipleship: Who am I because I belong to Jesus Christ? NOT generic
self-esteem. Ground every claim in union with Christ, grace, redemption, adoption,
justification, sanctification, membership in God's people.

## Guardrails
- Address false identity sources: achievement, career, money, appearance,
  relationship status, failure, shame, approval of others, ministry success.
- No claims of present sinless perfection: "new creation" and "free from slavery
  to sin" coexist with ongoing struggle and repentance (Romans 7–8, 1 John 1:8-9);
  sanctification is a process ("being conformed").
- Adoption/"child of God" language: sensitive to readers with painful family
  histories; God is not a projection of an earthly parent.
- "Chosen": avoid a denominational argument about election; stay with what
  Ephesians 1 says (in Christ, for holiness and praise).
- Day on "your past is not your master": grace without minimising harm done; may
  point to confession, restitution, counsel. Safety note if shame/self-harm
  thoughts: pointing to a pastor/counsellor/emergency help briefly.
