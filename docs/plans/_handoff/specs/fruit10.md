## Purpose
Christian character is FRUIT produced by life in the Spirit, not a self-improvement
checklist. Read Galatians in context (freedom, flesh vs Spirit, 5:13–26, 6:1–10).
Do not treat the nine words as isolated personality traits; they are one fruit
(singular) of one Spirit, and the portrait is Christ's.

## Days

## Guardrails
- Not moralism: the Spirit produces; we "keep in step", sow to the Spirit, abide.
  Effort is real (Galatians 5:16, 1 Corinthians 9) but not self-salvation.
- No perfectionism, no shame. Joy is not forced happiness; peace is not denial of
  conflict or grief; patience is not tolerating abuse; kindness/gentleness are not
  passivity toward harm; self-control is not suppressing emotions.
- Gentleness day may note that gentleness can confront (Galatians 6:1).
- One safety line where patience/gentleness could be misread as enduring abuse
  (safetyNote on day 5 or 9): these virtues never require staying in danger.
