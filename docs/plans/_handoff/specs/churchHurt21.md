## Purpose
Help readers bring wounds connected to churches, ministry environments or
Christian leaders before God; lament honestly; distinguish Christ from human
failure; understand forgiveness, justice and boundaries; and cautiously
rediscover Christian community where appropriate. SENSITIVE PLAN — the most
safeguarding-critical of the 13.

## Non-negotiable principles (encode them in the text AND the tests)
- forgiveness is not denial;
- forgiveness does not automatically restore trust;
- forgiveness does not require continued access;
- reconciliation may not be appropriate or possible;
- reporting abuse is not unforgiveness;
- spiritual authority never excuses abuse or coercion;
- do not instruct anyone to return to an unsafe church;
- do not frame leaving abuse as rebellion against God;
- criminal conduct must not be concealed under church discipline;
- pastoral care, safeguarding professionals, counselling, medical care, legal
  help, law enforcement or emergency services may be appropriate.
- The hurt may range from disappointment and conflict to spiritual, emotional,
  sexual or financial abuse — the plan must serve the whole range without
  minimising the severe end or dramatising the mild end.
- The reader may be a leader who caused hurt, or someone both hurt and hurting —
  one day can gently name that, without shifting focus off the wounded.

## Passages
Psalm 34 (the brokenhearted), Psalm 55 (betrayal by a companion — v12-14),
Ezekiel 34 (God against shepherds who exploit the flock), Jeremiah 23:1-4,
Matthew 11:28-30, Matthew 18:15-20 handled carefully (not a script to force a
victim to confront an abuser; also Matthew 18:6 on causing little ones to
stumble), Matthew 23 (Jesus confronts religious leaders), Mark 10:42-45, John 10
(the Good Shepherd vs hired hand/thief), Romans 12:17-21 (vengeance belongs to God
— and He uses governing authority, Romans 13:3-4), Ephesians 4:25-32, 1 Peter
5:1-4 (shepherds not lording it over), 3 John 9-11 (Diotrephes), Psalm 13,
Psalm 142, Luke 4:18, 2 Corinthians 1:3-4, Hebrews 13:17 ONLY with care (never as
leverage), Acts 20:28-30.
Do NOT weaponize Hebrews 10:25 against wounded readers (if used, only as gentle
invitation with explicit permission to take time and choose a safe community).

## Safety notes
Use `safetyNote` on several days: at least day 1 (if you are in danger or were
abused, you deserve safety and help — name emergency services, police,
safeguarding bodies, trusted counsellors; abuse of a child or a vulnerable adult
must be reported), the forgiveness days, the reporting/justice day, the
return-to-community days. Calm, plain, not diagnostic.
