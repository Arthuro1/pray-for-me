# children21 — Praying for Your Children & the Next Generation

- file: `prayingForChildren` · export `PRAYING_FOR_CHILDREN` · category `relationships` · mode prayer · 21 days
- resourceDomains: `['relationships']` · keys prefix `planChildren`
- movements (fixed): `belonging` 1–5 "Belonging to God" · `character` 6–10
  "Character and wisdom" · `relationships` 11–15 "Relationships and protection" ·
  `calling` 16–21 "Calling, faith and surrender"
- Do NOT set `lifeStage` (the plan is for parents, grandparents, guardians,
  mentors, spiritual parents, godparents, teachers, any Christian).

## Purpose
Help adults pray biblically for children and the next generation. Core theme:
children ultimately belong to God; adults are stewards, not owners of their future.
The plan must NOT imply faithful prayer guarantees conversion, health, safety,
academic success, marriage, vocation, prosperity or a particular future.
The title/intro must welcome those without their own children (spiritual
parenting, the next generation in their church) and must not wound those who
long for children or have lost a child (a gentle line in intro is enough).

## Themes (spread across the movements)
children as gifts entrusted by God; knowing Christ personally; loving Scripture;
wisdom; integrity; conscience; identity rooted in Christ; friendships; teachers and
mentors; protection; courage under pressure; sexuality and purity handled without
shame; digital/media influences; emotional maturity; gifts and abilities; future
vocation; compassion; church community; future relationships; future generations;
surrendering the child to God.

## Passages (in context)
Psalm 127, Psalm 78:1-8, Deuteronomy 6:4-9, Proverbs 1–4, Mark 10:13-16, Luke 2:40,
Luke 2:52, Ephesians 6:1-4, 2 Timothy 1:5, 2 Timothy 3:14-17; also Psalm 139,
Psalm 145:4, Joel 1:3, 1 Samuel 1:27-28 (Hannah's surrender — descriptive),
Daniel 1 (young people under pressure), 1 Timothy 4:12, Matthew 18:1-6, 10.
Do NOT treat Proverbs (esp. 22:6) as an unconditional promise — say so where used.

## Guardrails
- Every day: `selfPrompt` mirror for the adult (e.g. "pray for your own example").
- Prompts pray FOR children, never for control over them; respect the child's own
  personhood and eventual choices.
- Sexuality/purity day: without shame; age-appropriate; protection from
  exploitation. Protection day: safetyNote — if a child is being harmed or at risk,
  prayer goes with action: contact child-protection services, police or emergency
  services; follow safeguarding procedures (no details of any child's situation
  needed in the app).
- Digital day: wisdom, not fear.
- No gendered stereotypes about boys/girls.
- Grief-sensitive: a child who has died, estranged adult children, children not
  (yet) believing — the plan does not blame parents.
