## Purpose
Biblical womanhood as a life submitted to God and increasingly conformed to
Christ. NOT primarily a wife/mother plan. Deeply useful to single women, married
women, women without children, mothers, young adults, older women, women working
outside the home, women working in the home, and women serving in church and
community. It must be a genuinely distinct curriculum from manOfGod21 (another
agent writes that) — built on biblical women and women's specific pressures,
temptations and questions — not the same plan with pronouns swapped.

## Shared foundation (days 1–5 especially)
created in God's image; sinners needing grace; united with Christ; indwelt by the
Spirit; called to holiness, wisdom, courage, service; gifted; members of Christ's body.

## Themes
image of God; daughterhood; identity in Christ; dignity; wisdom; strength;
courage; holiness; sexuality; freedom from comparison; freedom from
appearance-centred identity; friendship; work; gifts; influence; service;
discernment; spiritual motherhood; motherhood without defining womanhood by
motherhood; suffering; perseverance; women serving in God's redemptive story;
finishing faithfully.

## Biblical women to study in context (choose ~12–16, one focus per day)
Eve, Sarah, Hagar (the God who sees — Genesis 16:13), Miriam, Deborah, Ruth,
Hannah, Abigail, Esther, Mary, Elizabeth, Anna, Mary Magdalene, the women who
supported Jesus (Luke 8:1-3), the Samaritan woman (John 4), Martha and Mary
(Luke 10:38-42, John 11), Lydia (Acts 16), Priscilla (Acts 18), Phoebe (Romans
16:1-2), women in Romans 16, Euodia and Syntyche (Philippians 4:2-3), Dorcas
(Acts 9), the Shulammite? (avoid), Rahab, Jochebed/Shiphrah and Puah (Exodus 1).
Narrative is descriptive: draw lessons without turning every detail into a rule.

## Proverbs 31
Do not reduce it to domestic performance. Portray her as strong, economically
active, generous, wise, prepared, industrious, God-fearing; note it is an acrostic
poem of praise for wisdom embodied (the "woman of valour"), not a checklist every
woman must meet (read with Ruth 3:11). No perfectionism, no comparison.

## Owner decision on disputed roles (follow exactly)
Shared ground only. Where a text (1 Peter 3, Ephesians 5, 1 Timothy 2, Titus 2)
raises headship/submission/church-office questions, name fairly that Christians
read it differently (complementarian and egalitarian readings) and do not
adjudicate; keep to what they share. Submission is never to abuse (safetyNote).
Flag such days in the research note.

## Avoid defining womanhood by (write tests)
appearance, marriage, motherhood, passivity, dependence on male approval.
Also avoid: women as temptresses, women as emotionally weaker, "a woman's worth is
her husband/children", singleness as a waiting room, infertility as failure.
