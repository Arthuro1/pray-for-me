Pray4Me — Privacy Policy

Your personal prayer companion

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Overview

Pray4Me is a prayer journal and discipleship companion. This page explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. We collect the minimum data needed to run the app and never sell your data to third parties.

Account data

When you create an account we store your email address and authentication credentials with our database provider, Supabase. This is used solely to sign you in and identify your data.

Prayer content

Prayers and journal entries you keep private are end-to-end encrypted on your device before being stored. Only you hold the decryption key (protected by your passphrase and an optional recovery code) — we cannot read the content of your private prayers or testimonies.

If you choose to share a prayer with a community or group, that specific content is stored as plain text so the people you shared it with can read it. Anything you have not explicitly shared stays private and encrypted.

AI-assisted features (optional)

Pray4Me can offer AI-generated guidance and scripture suggestions using Anthropic's Claude API. This feature is opt-in: we ask for your explicit consent before ever sending anything to the AI. When you consent, only the specific information needed (such as a prayer's title or a category name) is sent server-side to Anthropic for generating a response — never your encrypted private content, and never without your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.

Scripture content

Bible verse text may be fetched from the YouVersion API (where enabled) or generated via the AI provider above, and cached to reduce repeated requests.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, we store a push notification subscription (via the standard Web Push protocol) used only to deliver the reminders you configure. You can disable this at any time in your device or browser settings.

Feedback

If you submit feedback through the in-app feedback form, the message you write is stored so we can read and act on it. Only submit information you're comfortable sharing with the Pray4Me team.

Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights to understand aggregate usage and performance (e.g. which pages are used, load times). This data is anonymized and not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Donations

The in-app donation link takes you to an external PayPal page. Pray4Me does not process, see, or store any payment or card information.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use the following service providers (sub-processors) to run the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, storage
  • Anthropic — AI guidance, only for content you consent to send
  • Vercel — hosting and aggregate analytics
  • YouVersion — scripture text, where enabled

Your rights

You can delete your prayers, testimonies, or account at any time from within the app. To request a full export or deletion of your data, or ask any question about this policy, contact us at arthur.meteng@gmail.com.

Children

Pray4Me is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.
