# Background prayer instrumentals

These first-party instrumental files power the music selector inside every
prayer session. They are served from Praystead's own origin; selecting or changing
a track sends no prayer content to an external music service.

Only add tracks the project has the legal right to distribute. Keep them
instrumental, calm, and suitable for seamless looping. The public filenames and
localized labels are declared in `src/lib/audio/backgroundAudio.js`.

Music is optional. The selector always includes a real **Silence** choice, and a
missing or blocked audio file must never prevent a prayer session from working.
