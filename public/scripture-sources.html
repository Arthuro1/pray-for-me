Presentation of excerpts

Scripture wording is preserved. Formatting conversion removes presentation markup, headings, notes, editorial cross-references and printed alternative verse labels, and normalizes whitespace. It does not translate, paraphrase or rewrite verses.

References use Western chapter and verse numbering. Verified mappings account for edition-specific Psalm numbering. When an edition combines adjacent verses, its complete wording appears once with the full verse range.
