## Intent

A short silent video for the Qetoret WhatsApp channel explaining why the app
supports these 16 languages. Same visual family as the 1.0.6 release video
(videos/qetoret-1-0-6). Cuts in EN, DE and FR via a `lang` variable.

The four criteria, in the user's words: "the size of the Christian community,
where the church is growing, persecuted believers, a shared language across a
region". Japanese gets its own short line: one of the world's most spoken
languages (user's suggestion, confirmed by research: ~13th by total speakers).

## Customizations

- Mix of real app screens (Amharic, Arabic right to left) and moving text.
- Numbers on screen, each with its source: 2.3 billion Christians (Pew 2025),
  31% in sub-Saharan Africa (Pew 2025), 388 million facing high persecution
  (Open Doors WWL 2026; North Korea 1, Iran 10, India 12, China 17),
  Kiswahili 200 million+ speakers (UNESCO), Japanese ~123 million (Britannica,
  Ethnologue data).
- Persecution named openly (countries named).
- Revelation 7:9 quoted word for word from a public-domain Bible, version
  labeled: WEB (en), Louis Segond 1910 (fr), Elberfelder 1871 (de; the user
  turned down Luther 1912 because it reads "Heiden"). The user checks
  the text before rendering.
- Honest line: the translation work is still growing and some texts still need
  a check by native speakers.
- Call to action for native speakers; the video says the details are in the
  message, the contact lives only in the WhatsApp text.
- Ending as in 1.0.6 (Android → Google Play, iPhone and computer →
  qetoret.com); no "change language in Settings".
- WhatsApp message per language: small and ready to copy.

## Notes

- Never say "nothing to install" in release copy.

- "Brazil and the Philippines are among the largest Christian nations" is left
