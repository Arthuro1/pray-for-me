## English

*Qetoret 1.0.6: what's new*

A quiet space to pray in the Spirit, prayer times for your groups, and more.

🕊️ *Pray in the Spirit*
One quiet space to pray freely, with or without words. Choose a length or no timer; the screen stays on.

🗓️ *Praying together*
Schedule a prayer time for your group, in person or online. Members get a reminder 15 minutes before and can join the call or add it to their calendar.

⛪ *Your church and the Lord's day*
Name your local church, kept private and encrypted. On Sunday, Today helps you prepare for worship, then bring before God what you received.

⭕ *Clearer circle pages*
Each theme opens with its meaning, a reflection and Scripture, all on one page.

📲 *Get the update*
Android: install or update the app on Google Play 👉 https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=space.praystead.twa
iPhone and computer: open qetoret.com in your browser 👉 https://qetoret.com

Un seul espace calme pour prier librement, avec ou sans mots. Choisissez une durée, ou aucune ; l'écran reste allumé.

Chaque thème s'ouvre sur son sens, une réflexion et l'Écriture, sur une seule page.
